Orbit Daily Studio

Orbit Daily Studio is a small personal tool that makes short educational videos about space and science and posts them to its owner's own accounts.

  • Every video is original, narrated by a synthetic voice, and labelled as AI-generated content where the platform provides that label.
  • Footage comes from public-domain and openly licensed sources such as NASA. Sources are listed in each video's description.
  • The tool posts only to accounts its owner controls. It has no other users and does not post on anyone else's behalf.
  • It reads view counts for its own videos so the owner can see which topics work.
