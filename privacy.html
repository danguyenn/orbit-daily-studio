Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 November 2026

Orbit Daily Studio is a single-owner tool. It has no users other than its owner and does not collect personal data from visitors or viewers.

What it accesses. After the owner authorizes it, the tool accesses only the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts: the basic profile, the list of the owner's videos, and the view, like and comment counts of those videos. It uses this to publish the owner's videos and to see which topics perform.

Storage. Access tokens and video statistics are stored on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

This website. It is a static page. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics.

Revoking access. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in the TikTok and Google account security settings.
